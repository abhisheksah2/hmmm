About This Project

🎯 Purpose

The Hinglish Translator bridges the gap between English and Hindi languages by translating common English idioms into their equivalent Hindi proverbs (Kahawaten). This helps learners understand cultural nuances and language expressions.

📚 What is Hinglish?

Hinglish is a blend of Hindi and English, commonly used in modern India. This tool helps you appreciate the richness of both languages while making translations more accessible and meaningful.

💡 How to Use

Simply type an English idiom in the search box, click search, and instantly see its Hindi equivalent along with meanings and usage examples. You can also browse by category or get random idioms.
